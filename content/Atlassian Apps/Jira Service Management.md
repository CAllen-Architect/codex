Jira Service Management is a platform built on Jira with a few key differences.

- It features a Portal View allowing Customers who would not normally have permissions to interact with the issue on a Limited Basis
	- This includes a "Internal/External Comment System for Agents"
	- This includes a "Request Participant" System allowing people to become "Co-Customers"
- SLA Timers
